4. **Publish & share.** Choose your link, publish, and share it however you

Your invitation stays live for **18 months** after you publish it — more

**No Canva needed — ever**

Unlike Canva-based wedding website templates, nothing can break when you
resize a photo or move a text box. Every layout is guided and tested on
real phones. Colors — including on illustrations like the envelope, wax
seal and stamps — are genuinely customizable, not fixed images.

  basic form.
- _What if I need help?_ Message us anytime through Etsy — we're happy to
  help with activation or customization questions.
