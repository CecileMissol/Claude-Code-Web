## 2. FAQ replies (copy-paste for common questions)

**"How long does delivery/activation take?"**

> Your download is instant — Etsy delivers the PDF guide as soon as you
> check out. Activating your editor access takes one extra step: enter
> your order number and email at {{BRAND_URL}}/activate, and we confirm it
> within 24 hours (usually much faster). You'll get an email the moment
> it's ready.

**"What's your refund policy?"**

> Because this is a digital product delivered instantly, we can offer a
> full refund within 24 hours of purchase if you haven't activated your
> invitation yet and change your mind. Once you've activated and started
> customizing, we're not able to refund for a change of mind, but we will
> always fix a genuine technical issue for free — just tell us what's
> happening first.

**"Can I make changes after my invitation is published?"**

> Yes, anytime — and there's no limit. Log in at {{BRAND_URL}}/app, open
> your invitation, and edit any section. Changes go live immediately on
> your existing link, so your guests always see the latest version.

**"I need more than one invitation — can I order multiple?"**

> Yes — each order activates one invitation. If you need a second one (for
> example a separate save-the-date and full invitation, or two different
> events), just place a second order and activate it with its own order
> number; you'll get a second, independent link and dashboard.

**"Can I get named/personalized links for each guest?"**

> Not in the base listing — the base invitation gives you one shared link
> for all your guests. If you'd like a personal link per household (their
> name shown on the envelope, their own headcount), check our "Named
> invitations" add-on listing — it plugs into your existing invitation, no
> need to start over.
